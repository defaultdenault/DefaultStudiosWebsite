Sweeper's Luck OUT NOW

"A Minesweeper roguelike about greed. Deduce which tiles are safe, then bet on the ones worth taking."

Clear the board the way you always have — then do the part Minesweeper never let you do. Load your safe tiles onto Slips and score them as poker hands, multiplied by the patterns you managed to carve out of the grid. Every run asks the same question: bank what you have, or go back in for one more tile.

Build a deck from 100 Lucky Stickers, 35 Tokens and 25 board upgrades across endless runs with branching routes and boss encounters. Deduction gets you the safe tiles. Greed decides how far you get.
