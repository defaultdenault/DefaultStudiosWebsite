Before you write

It helps enormously if you can include:

  • Which project you are writing about.
  • Your operating system and, if relevant, your hardware.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Any error message, copied exactly, or a screenshot.
⚠ Heads up: we will never ask you for a password, a payment detail or a licence key over email. If a message claiming to be from us does, it is not from us.
