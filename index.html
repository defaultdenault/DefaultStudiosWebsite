★ SWEEPER'S LUCK IS OUT NOW ON STEAM ★ NEW!
September 10, 2026 — Posted by the Webmaster

It is finally out. Sweeper's Luck is a Minesweeper roguelike about greed: deduce which tiles are safe, then bet on the ones worth taking.
